# Getting Started with NereusSDR

NereusSDR is under active development. This guide will be updated as features are implemented.

## Requirements

- An OpenHPSDR Protocol 1 or Protocol 2 compatible radio
- Network connection to the radio (Ethernet)

## Connecting to Your Radio

1. Launch NereusSDR
2. Open **Radio > Connections…** (in some windows, **Radio > Manage Radios…**)
   and find your radio under the radios on your network. Press **Scan** if it
   is not listed yet.
3. Select your radio and press **Connect**. The Connections picker closes once the radio
   connects; if it stays open, read the reason it shows.

The [user guide](https://nereussdr.com/manual/) covers connecting through a
Core, the phone app and everything after the first connection.

## Supported Radios

- Apache Labs ANAN line (ANAN-G2, ANAN-7000DLE, ANAN-8000DLE, ANAN-200D, etc.)
- Hermes Lite 2
- All OpenHPSDR Protocol 1 and Protocol 2 compatible radios
