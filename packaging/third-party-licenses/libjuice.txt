NereusSDR uses libjuice through libdatachannel as the R3 direct ICE backend.
The pinned source is fetched from:

  https://github.com/paullouisageneau/libjuice/tree/3c40a3545b6b1b62c7adee7f8f2bd58aa290afd6

libjuice is licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0. The complete
license is supplied in MPLv2.txt. This backend does not supply the R5 TURN
TCP/TLS fallback; that deployment acceptance remains separate.

Modification (MPL-2.0 section 3.1): NereusSDR compiles one covered file,
src/agent.c, with a change of its own. When an ICE agent is destroyed it
gives each TURN allocation back with a Refresh of LIFETIME 0 (RFC 8656
section 7.2). The change is the source file
cmake/patches/libjuice-release-turn-allocations.c in the NereusSDR source,
which cmake/NereusRemoteMedia.cmake inserts into a copy of agent.c in the
build tree (nereus_patch_libjuice_turn_release); that source is available
under the same terms as the rest of NereusSDR's (SOURCE-OFFER.txt).
